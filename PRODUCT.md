# Product: Cupcakes & Cucumbers website

## Platform
web

## Stack
Static single-file HTML/CSS/JS (`index.html`), no frameworks, no tracking, system font stack. Hosted on GitHub Pages.

## Users
- Parents (mostly moms, inferred from the caption's tone: "You sit back, relax, and watch your child have the best time of their life!") planning a party for a young girl in New York or New Jersey.
- Most arrive on a phone from a Facebook group post or Instagram, inside an in-app browser (inferred: the business markets only there).

## Purpose
Give the owner, Marissa, a real website where there is none, and turn parents into party inquiries through the channel she uses today: her phone, 347-620-8903.

## Positioning
"Luxury At-Home Spa Parties for Little Divas" (tagline, verbatim). A mobile service that brings the whole spa party to the family's home and handles it end to end: "We travel. We set up. We host. We clean up."

## Capabilities
- Spa services (verbatim): Facials, Manicures, Pedicures, Hairstyling, Make-Up.
- Add-ons (verbatim): crafts, homemade cupcakes, hair pieces "& more".
- Mini Bars, bookable on their own or added to any party.
- Table & Chair Rentals; "Ultimate Sleepover Teepee Rentals".
- Occasions from the flyer (read on the image, not the OCR): birthday parties, fall get togethers, Halloween parties, craft nights, girls nights, sleepovers, Friendsgiving gatherings. Flyer tiles also show karaoke, crafts, "Glampagne", homemade cupcakes.
- Published promo: "10% OFF ALL PARTIES BOOKED BETWEEN NOW & THE END OF 2026!"
- Serving New York & New Jersey (no towns stated).

## Constraints
- No logo file (the flyer has a script wordmark: "Cupcakes" in pink, "Cucumbers" in sage green). No usable photos: the flyer and Instagram profile picture show children's faces, which stay off the page without the owner's consent.
- Accepts texts: unknown. Calls are the known channel; texting is offered as a pre-filled convenience, not the only path.
- No email, prices, hours, reviews or business Facebook page. Instagram @cupcakes_and_cucumbers is live (1,030 followers, 184 posts, seen 2026-10-17) and is the "see more" link.
- In business about one year ("LET'S END OUR FIRST YEAR WITH A BANG!").

## Brand commitments
- Playful, warm, girly, "luxury" in a pretend-spa way; pink and cucumber green from the flyer wordmark.
- Owner's voice, "we" (the caption uses "We travel. We set up...").
- Safety-conscious and reassuring for parents.

## Evidence on hand
- Facebook group post 2026-10-18 caption and flyer image (via Muse).
- Instagram profile meta (name, follower and post counts).

## Principles
- Phone first: the first screen shows a spa party at home, for NY & NJ, and how to book.
- Only facts from the brief, the flyer and the Instagram profile. Nothing invented.
- The page should feel like the party itself: cucumber slices, bubbles, pink.
